Visual Communication Poster

This is a poster made with the software Adobe Illustrator, using no online elements/images. It was made to visually communicate a concept, in this case the concept of "Reducing Greenhouse Gas Emissions". The poster was made for the course "Design for Behaviour Change", which is a course in the first year of Creative Technology. The goal of the course is to design in a way that can change the behaviour of people.
